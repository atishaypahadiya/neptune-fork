02 · THE PULL

Too faint to feel. Strong enough to bend an orbit

In 1822 the unseen planet passed about 11 AU from Uranus. Its tug was roughly three ten-billionths of Earth's gravity. Over decades, that was enough to pull Uranus 143″ off its path.
