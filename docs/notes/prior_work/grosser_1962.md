## Claims

1. The book is said to be based on Grosser's Ph.D. dissertation of 1961. [not read: Kollerstrom
   2006, Part 1, note 20]
2. The book is said to cite the first letter of the Sedgwick–Airy correspondence of December
   1846 and to state that the criticism "had almost no effect on Airy" (p. 137); its research
   is said to predate the disappearance of the Greenwich "Neptune file" in the mid-1960s, to
   show no sign of having used that file, and to have used the St John's College archives.
   [not read: Kollerstrom 2006, Part 2, note 200, read as OCR]
3. Le Verrier's final elements as this repository uses them (a = 36.15 AU, e = 0.10761, and
   the rest) are given by Krajnović 2016, Table 2, "after Grosser 1962". [not read: the
   attribution is Krajnović's, recorded in `docs/notes/M5.md` and `docs/notes/M6.md`; I did not
   reopen Krajnović for this note]
4. Whether the book contains any computation (a refit, a residual table, a test of the
   predictions) I cannot say. [not read]

## What it means for this repository

The repository takes Le Verrier's elements through Krajnović from this book and cites it on the
site. Since I have not read it, nothing in `novelty.md` may lean on what it does or does not
contain; it is a narrative history by reputation only, and that reputation is not a reading.
