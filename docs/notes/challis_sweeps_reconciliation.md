renders), `challis_sweeps_pass2.csv` (111 statements, an independent reading of the same 21 renders
under a written brief, with the 12 October letter transcribed sentence by sentence). Comparison:

"48s·9", which the tokeniser splits). Pass 2 reported no cell marked uncertain.
