## What was transcribed, and what was not

Four tables: I November transits, interior contacts (25 rows); II May transits, interior
contacts (12); III November transits, exterior contacts (15); IV May transits, exterior
contacts (6). Columns taken: contact (II or III; tables III and IV have none), date,
observed Greenwich mean time of geocentric contact (h m s, astronomical day from noon) with
its term in k, and weight.

**Not transcribed, on purpose:** the tabular times and, in tables I and II, the symbolic
corrections to the elements printed to the right. They are computed-side quantities, and
observed minus tabular is the anomaly itself. The page bands used for both entries are cut
so that those columns are not on them (`docs/phase2/scripts/mercury_crops.py`).
